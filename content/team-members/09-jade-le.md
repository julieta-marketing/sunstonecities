## Full Introduction

Jade Le holds a Master of Public Policy from the University of California, Riverside, with a concentration in business and environmental policy. She has experience leading government and private programs and initiatives in the green technology sector. At Sunstone Cities, Jade will bring her intersectoral experience, policy expertise, and commitment to economic development to the Project Financing team. She will serve as a central coordinator for public infrastructure projects and private financing transactions, guiding them from initial opportunity through financial close.
